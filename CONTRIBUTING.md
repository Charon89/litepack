# Contributing to LitePack

LitePack is not accepting outside contributions (pull requests with code, documentation or data) at this time,
until the owner decides how outside work will be taken in. Issues and bug reports are welcome.

When contributions open, these rules apply:

- A contribution is made under the project's licence, `Apache-2.0 OR MIT` (the files `LICENSE-APACHE` and
  `LICENSE-MIT`), unless the crate it changes states another licence (`crates/lpk-codecs-vp8` is Apache-2.0
  only; `vendor/` keeps each upstream's licence).
- The owner may relicense the owner's own code, including in later closed-source versions. That does not change
  the licence of anything already released, which stays available under `Apache-2.0 OR MIT`.
- An outside contribution keeps the licence it was contributed under; it is never relicensed without its author's
  agreement, and a version that includes it carries its attribution.
- No code may be copied or ported from GPL or LGPL projects (see `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/LICENSING.md`).
